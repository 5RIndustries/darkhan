# Darkhan — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 31, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 31, 2026

## The Short Version

Darkhan is self-hosted software. Your data stays on your machine. We never see it.

## What We Collect

**From your Darkhan instance:** Nothing. Zero. No telemetry, no analytics, no usage metrics, no crash reports, no phone-home. Your instance does not communicate with 5R Industries in any way.

**From darkhan.ai (our website):** We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party tracking. The site uses no cookies except standard session cookies if you log in to a self-hosted instance. We do not track visitors.

**Voluntarily provided information:** If you sign up for our mailing list or contact us, we store your email address to respond and send updates. That's it. You can unsubscribe anytime by emailing privacy@5rindustries.com.

## What We Do NOT Do

- Collect or process data from your Darkhan instance
- Sell, share, or monetize anyone's personal information
- Use third-party ad networks or tracking pixels
- Profile visitors to darkhan.ai

## Third Parties

We use GitHub to host the source code. GitHub's privacy policy applies to your interactions with that repository. We have no control over GitHub's data practices.

## Your Rights

Want your email removed from our mailing list? Email us. We will delete it. No hoops.
